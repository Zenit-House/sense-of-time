# Sense of Time — launch video script

Length: 30 s. Narration and on-screen text in English.
Motion graphic only: typography, shapes, chat bubbles.
Audio from ElevenLabs only; no generated images or video.

Tagline: **Your AI knows the time. But it can't FEEL the time.**

## 1. The problem (0–9 s)

VO:
> Your AI knows what time it is. But step away for a second, or a month.
> To the model, it's the same. You never left.

Screen:
- A digital clock ticking, then shrinks to a corner.
- Two chat bubbles. The label between them reads `1 second`, then morphs to
  `1 month`. The gap between the bubbles collapses to zero.
- Text: `To an LLM, both look the same.`

## 2. Why it matters (9–17 s)

VO:
> Time is information. Old context should fade. Unless it mattered: that
> four-hour session you poured yourself into deserves a second look.

Screen:
- A stack of context cards with dates. Old ones dim and sink.
- One old card stays bright, badge `4 h · 60 turns`, and rises above the rest.
- Text: `Recency × Intensity`.

## 3. What you get (17–25 s)

VO:
> Back after a week? A recap first. Past midnight? Shorter. Three hours
> straight? One question: still going? The rest of the time, silence.

Screen, three quick panels:
- `7 days later` → reply bubble opening with a recap line.
- `00:14` → a visibly shorter reply bubble.
- `3 h straight` → reply bubble: `Still going?`
- Text: `Time shapes how it answers. Not what it talks about.`

## 4. The product (25–30 s)

VO:
> Sense of Time. It stores when. Never what. Coming soon to GitHub and MCP.

Screen:
- Tagline, full frame: `Your AI knows the time. But it can't FEEL the time.`
- Wordmark: `sense-of-time`
- `Stores when. Never what.`
- `Coming soon to GitHub & MCP`
